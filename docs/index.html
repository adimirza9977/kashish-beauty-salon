    Privacy Policy

    How we handle your data

    What we collect. When you create an account or book an appointment, we collect your name, phone number and WhatsApp number, along with your booking details (service, date, time and payment reference).

    How we use it. Your details are used only to manage your bookings, confirm appointments, award loyalty points and send service updates on WhatsApp. We never sell your personal data to anyone.

    Your control. You can update your details by contacting the salon, and you can delete your account and personal data at any time from Profile › Delete My Account.

    Security. Passwords are stored as one-way encrypted hashes — even the salon cannot read them. Booking data is stored on secure cloud servers.

    Questions? Message us on WhatsApp at +92 312 7830765 and we will be happy to help.
